# V4 official regression: confirmed outcome, bounded diagnosis

Official submission968634 finished at0.1222, versus v2 submission968359 at0.3462. Root captured the exact rows and detail in `experiments/official_feedback1008_1628`; logs explicitly say they are withheld and no scoring/prediction downloads are enabled. V4 is rejected as a replacement, and v2 remains the best submitted incumbent. Neither the aggregate nor55 passing local controls identifies which families, evidence penalties, incomplete units or forecast changes caused the decline.

The two versions share byte-identical `analyze.py`. V4 replaces the House controller and adds concept-diverse evidence, typed issuer/period features, numerical priors, fictional procedural method cards and a second review per batch. It validates some explicit arithmetic/label relationships more strictly. These are several simultaneous mechanisms, so the official change is not a matched typed-feature ablation.

The read-only replay used all11 manifest-verified public units and78 entities, with a deterministic client that supplied only task-anchor/label-consistent synthetic JSON. Both exact runtimes completed all11. No House call, model-quality evaluation, outcome/reference access, runtime edit or publication occurred. Every full outgoing user prompt's byte count, hash, timeout and entity roster was captured in `replay_v2.json` and `replay_v4.json`; exact source and organizer input hashes were verified. These measurements include complete review prompts, their candidates and repeated evidence rather than just feature context.

The evidence expansion is uneven. The first credit prompt grows23,345→90,763bytes, the banks prompt23,694→88,810bytes, mixed earnings23,542→92,671bytes, and postearnings18,555→81,409bytes. Typed values and their exact header/row citations are accompanied by both evidence and reason-evidence text, and the review repeats that context again. COT adds full-roster priors; its first prompt grows19,322→39,913bytes. More source text may improve reading, but more bytes and twice the calls also impose work and possible distraction. These are measured inputs, not measured tokens, prefill time or a causal explanation of the official score.

V4 narrows per-request time despite that larger input. It divides remaining450seconds by twice the remaining batch count, while v2 divides530 by the remaining batch count, both capped90seconds. For30 entities/eight batches, first V4 timeout is28.125seconds and v2 is66.25seconds. A fixed synthetic30-entity virtual-delay tripwire completed v2 at20,30,45 and60seconds per response, using8 calls and160–480 virtualseconds. V4 completed only the20second case using16 calls/320seconds;30,45 and60seconds per response failed the first batch after two retries,54.4921875virtualseconds. A single failed batch raises before the complete answer is written. This demonstrates a concrete completion-risk path under fixed latency. It does **not** establish that official House responses took30seconds, that socket timeout measures whole-body duration, or that this path caused the observed0.1222.

Strict extraction is another possible mechanism, but existing public coverage does not show broad evidence deletion. Across78 entities, the issuer guard removed five original spans: one credit and four banks spans. Two earnings/credit entities have no typed features, while existing eligible raw evidence remains; period/scale abstentions skip typed rows rather than force a numeric forecast or make the House abstain. These refusals may be correct when legacy metadata or CIK binding conflicts. The right audit is to inspect each removed/zero-feature entity and verify issuer identity, required quarter and source table with exact citations, including failures rather than only JPM/AMD successes. Do not relax issuer/period guards merely to boost coverage.

The generic memory cards are fabricated **procedural examples**, not a mature library of independently measured financial cases. The earnings example subtracts a hypothetical transitory per-share gain, but no executable accounting bridge reconciles actual taxes, attribution, recurring income and share counts. The review accepts a valid evidence ID plus a specific-looking issue; that proves identity/provenance, not that a new forecast is more accurate. The v5 historical ranking failure already shows why more adaptation or smaller MAE cannot establish useful ordering. The official regression is compatible with harmful priors/reviews, excess completion cost, changed financial forecasts or other unseen family failures; none is currently confirmed.

The next useful experiment should split **completion** and **prediction**. First preserve exact v2's completion budget and one-pass forecast as the baseline, then measure one bounded information change with complete prompt bytes and latency. Require a full-roster output before elective review; a review must spend only measured remaining headroom and must preserve a complete parent on failure. Select a small evidence budget around the target quarter/denominator, with typed numbers linked to their exact source rather than repeated broad context. These are design hypotheses, not implemented fixes. A future genuine House replay should compare exact v2, v4 forecast before review, and v4 final review on the same representative frozen visible-document cohort, recording completion, revisions and harmful changes. Use independent labels to evaluate financial quality; contract echoing cannot promote a version.

The fixed all11 public replay is representative of currently supplied document/table formats but consumed practice data, so it cannot be an untouched competitive holdout. A separately frozen noncompetition financial cohort should cover failed/ambiguous extraction, near-identical issuer names, no guidance, net loss/one-offs, cumulative versus quarter numbers, refinancing mitigation, sparse histories, larger rosters and latency. Predeclare source/vintage/target semantics, output losses, complete-unit denominator and cost limits before model outcomes. Fit no mechanism from the hidden aggregate alone. Official review remains root's decision; no extra submission follows from this diagnosis.
