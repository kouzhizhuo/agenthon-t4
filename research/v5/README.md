## Executive summary (read this first)

Track 4 needs measurable predictive information as well as reliable evidence reading. This round defines one small positioning model that can learn common cross-market coefficients from older resolved observations without changing the House model. It compares that model with the frozen parent on a separate historical CFTC benchmark, with labels accessible only to the evaluator. A matched House comparison isolates the typed financial reading layer from the parent prompt and reviewer. No public practice unit supplies a future answer, and neither mechanical correctness nor extra extracted facts establish a forecast gain. This research uses zero competition submissions and preserves the frozen parent.

`PROTOCOL.json` fixes the mechanism, temporal boundaries, resource use, metrics and admission rule before accuracy is read. The numeric experiment uses futures-only noncommercial positions and divides future target net changes by the initial open interest. Momentum uses the current initial denominator; the weekly drift and crowding features are percentage-point proxies based on each historical report's own denominator. It shares three coefficients across markets, shrinks them with a fixed ridge penalty, and pools one quarter of the learned point with the parent. Fully resolved, nonoverlapping training windows and continuous report cadence prevent a new origin from learning a target that lies beyond its visible input. Release availability assumes Tuesday reports become available three days later on Friday; holiday and delayed releases require additional provenance before a fully point-in-time claim.

The independent historical benchmark spans 2012–2023, with development origins in 2015–2018 and reserved origins in 2019–2023. These are earlier periods of the published market families, so successful results would establish temporal transfer under those families, not broad unseen-family generalization. The historical source is separate from the competition's 2024 evidence and future targets. House runs need a valid local staging configuration and independently labeled EPS/credit cases before forecast quality is measurable.
