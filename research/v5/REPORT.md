## Executive summary (read this first)

The finite pooled-positioning model was rejected after one declared historical comparison. On reserved CFTC cases, it reduced numerical mean absolute error by about ten percent but made the primary cross-sectional ranking slightly worse. The parent and candidate both had weak ranking correlations and interval coverage near sixty-one percent rather than the declared ninety percent. Strict date guards left only twenty-one reserved cross-sections across three years, below the predeclared minimum. The experiment therefore supports preserving the parent and learning a method-level failure, not publishing a new candidate. No House call, production faithfulness evaluation or official submission of v5 occurred.

The immutable CFTC input consists of twelve source-observed annual legacy futures-only archives for 2012–2023. `benchmark/acquisition_plan.json` pins the official source page and exact links. `benchmark/market_mapping.json` fixes ten market codes before scoring, including E-mini S&P 500 rather than a consolidated or large-contract alias. `benchmark/panel_provenance.json` records every archive and member digest, 6,190 reconstructed input rows, stable renamed market labels and seventy non-Tuesday holiday exclusions. Nothing from 2024–2026 was downloaded or used.

`benchmark/BENCHMARK_FREEZE.json` pins twenty-six source, method, panel and reservation files before accuracy. Independent source review reconstructed all panel rows exactly and verified dates, five-week targets, initial-open-interest outcomes and nonoverlapping origins. The strict weekly cadence and sixty-report history guard retained twenty-eight development cross-sections in 2015–2018 and twenty-one reserved cross-sections in 2020, 2022 and 2023. Holiday exclusions and gaps explain why the reserved sample cannot meet the declared forty-five-origin, four-year admission requirement. That requirement was not relaxed after source inspection or scores.

The model preserves the parent's cross-section mean and learns relative changes from three historical proxies. Its shrinkage lowers the magnitude of some errors without adding reliable information about which market's net positioning will rise most. Lower regression error is therefore insufficient for this ranking task. The intervals were preserved and sometimes expanded to contain the pooled point; this experiment did not fit a new uncertainty model. Unchanged interval loss and poor nominal coverage identify a separate calibration weakness rather than an improvement from memory.

`results/development.json` and `results/reserved.json` preserve the sole comparisons, complete case records, year-level paired uncertainty, fallbacks, exclusions and admission conditions. The benchmark treats Tuesday-plus-three-days as Friday availability; actual holiday and shutdown-delayed release timestamps are unavailable. It is a retrospective diagnostic on related market families, with only three reserved year clusters. Candidate inputs contain only earlier report rows, but these limits prevent a full point-in-time or unseen-competition-generalization claim. The evaluator was run with the exact frozen panel, and parent source hashes were independently verified; its generic CLI does not itself bind arbitrary input paths to that panel, which should be tightened before future benchmark reuse.

Independent saved-result review reproduced all source and result hashes, complete case/exclusion ledgers, equal-origin summaries, year mass, 5,000 seed-zero whole-year bootstrap samples and admission decisions with maximum arithmetic error zero. It performed no forecast rerun and changed no runtime. The review also checked recorded training targets against the visible history. `independent_results_track3_receipt.json` and `benchmark/audit_results_track3.py` pin that audit; `completion_manifest.json` records this finished rejected package separately from the preaccuracy freeze.

The useful memory entry is a transferable failure condition: a pooled historical model can improve numerical error while failing a ranking objective. A future method needs additional observable predictive information, a separately calibrated rank or interval model, and a new predeclared independent benchmark. The current benchmark must not become a source of unlimited post-hoc feature selection. For earnings and credit, the matched typed-feature House ablation remains independently executable but unrun because no usable local House or production judge configuration exists.

`NEXT_HYPOTHESIS.md` independently examines the rank objective, market-specific scaling and the distinction between observed COT movement and a resolved forecast innovation. It proposes only one bounded pairwise innovation correction and an evidence plan. It is unimplemented, does not reuse the consumed reserve as a holdout, and does not authorize another submission.
